## What Claude for Teams is

It is a paid, shared version of Claude for a company, instead of everyone using separate personal accounts. It comes in two seat types that can be mixed within the same organization.

**The one fact that decides who needs which seat: Claude Code — the tool used to build and maintain the CSG website and scheduler — only works on a Premium seat.** Standard seats cannot use it.

## Pricing

| Seat type | Price (billed annually) | Price (billed monthly) | Claude Code included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $20/seat/month | $25/seat/month | No |
| Premium | $100/seat/month | $125/seat/month | Yes |

Other terms:

- Team plans require a minimum of two members and support up to 150 seats.
- Standard and Premium seats can be mixed in one organization.
- Weekly usage limits reset at a fixed time assigned to the account.
- Usage credits can be prepaid so people can keep working after hitting a weekly limit.

## Recommended shape for CSG

Standard seats for office staff who use Claude for everyday writing and research tasks, and one Premium seat for whoever maintains the website and scheduler. Only the person maintaining these systems needs Claude Code, and that is the expensive seat — there is no reason to pay the Premium rate for people who are not touching the codebases.
